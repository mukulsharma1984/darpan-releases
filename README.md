# Darpan (दर्पण) — downloads

Darpan is a free Vedic astrology (Jyotish) app for Windows. It shows the real sky at your birth and today, your kundali with divisional charts, your Vimshottari dasha periods, and written answers for 39 areas of life (health, wealth, career, marriage, children, education and more), in English and Hindi.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/mukulsharma1984/darpan-releases/releases/latest)**. On that page, click **Darpan-Setup-x.y.z.exe**.

## Install

1. Run the downloaded `Darpan-Setup-x.y.z.exe`.
2. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the installer is not code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Darpan installs for your user account only (no administrator rights needed) and adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts.
4. Open Darpan, go to **Profiles** → **New birth chart**, and enter the birth details.

To remove it: Windows Settings → Apps → Darpan → Uninstall.

## Your data

- Birth details and settings stay on your PC, in `%APPDATA%\Darpan\darpan.json`. Darpan does not upload them anywhere.
- The app works offline. It goes online only if you use **Search online** for a place, or if you add your own AI key in Settings. Even then, the AI receives only the question and the chart facts — never a name, birth date or birthplace.
